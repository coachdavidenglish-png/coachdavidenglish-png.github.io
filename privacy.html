Privacy Policy

David English Uploader - last updated October 26, 2026

David English Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by David English Career Coach. It is used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos to the David English Career Coach YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and publish times, and to read basic channel information (the channel name) to confirm the right channel is connected.

What the app stores

The app stores a sign-in token only on the owner's own computer so uploads can run. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not access data from any other YouTube user.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.
